FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still have questions? Email Kelvin.

Who maintains the unit?

Kelvin runs the Compute — the unit and the cooling. You remain the Energy host: site access, the connection, and the Heat sink where you have one.

How is Flexibility-as-a-Service paid?

Paid on stand-by, on activation, and while Compute is running. See How it pays.

Why not batteries?

Batteries store Energy. Compute earns while it flexes. Multiple revenues, continuous dispatch, no cycle wear. vs Battery Flexibility.

Retrofit or plug-and-play?

Low-demand retrofit, high-demand plug-and-play.

What happens when Compute pauses?

The load is built to ramp. When the grid is tight or the surplus disappears, jobs checkpoint and the site steps down. Heat follows the load — a paused unit is not a baseload boiler.

Who takes the Heat?

Local on site, municipal or district. We do not take over the network. Thermal.

Why Sweden and the Nordics?

The north spills cheap power; the south imports expensive. Kelvin sits the load at that surplus. Energy.

It’s not a Wires problem. It’s a Flexibility problem. We solve it.

Do we need a new grid connection?

No. Behind the meter, at the surplus. Energy.

Does this only work as a full campus?

No. One Flexbox is a valid first step. Scale.

Is Bitcoin a product?

Compute that flexes. Paid on stand-by, paid on activation, paid while active and paid in yield. No matter the market price of Bitcoin, we produce for Production Price. Bitcoin.
