Why it holds

It’s a Compute Flexibility problem — not a wire problem.

Surplus is switched off because the load cannot move. Heat rides along.

  1. 01

    Milliseconds, not minutes

    Frequency needs a response in milliseconds. Interruptible Compute sheds on firmware signal — first off, first on. The Flexbox is built to move in <0.7 s.

  2. 02

    A pre-tested unit

    On-site controller and industrial-grade smart meters delivered as a single, pre-tested unit with the Compute. Kelvin software: control · dispatch · monitoring.

  3. 03

    The cheapest megawatt to switch off

    Interruptible Compute is the load that can switch off. Batch pauses at a checkpoint. The host never notices. See Compute.
