  3. Hydro

Why it holds

Sit the load at the surplus — not a new feeder.

  1. Queue

    The queue is years.

    Demand-connection queues run years.

    Containerised centres don’t require major grid reinforcements. A module sits where the Energy already is.

  2. North

    The north spills cheap.

    The cause is congestion. Northern hydro and wind cannot move south fast enough, so the north clears cheap and the south clears expensive.

    Power is 60–80% of the cost base. Sweden, 2025: 322 negative-price hours. First plots sit in Hälsingland.

  3. Local

    Energy, Compute and Thermal/Heat stay local.

    The same surplus. A module at the source. Compute. Thermal/Heat.

    The interruptible load is on Compute. See Compute Flexibility.

  4. Locked

    The site is the moat.

    Surplus sites, once locked, are not a copy-paste campus.

    Locked-in surplus cannot be copied.

See Compute vs Battery Flexibility.
