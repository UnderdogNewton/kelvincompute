Surplus

Two problems. One infrastructure.

Energy without a buyer. Compute without a place. When prices go negative, the load ramps up.

Moving Compute to where the electrons already are works on the timescale the grid needs. Kelvin absorbs that surplus where it is already spilling.

  1. 01

    Floor on output

    Compute behind the meter sets a floor on wind and solar output without sacrificing upside — curtailment becomes load.

  2. 02

    Curtailment into revenue

    Negative-price hours become earning opportunities. The Flexbox consumes generation that would otherwise be wasted.

  3. 03

    Uncorrelated income

    Compute income is uncorrelated with the spot price — a stable stream that cuts exposure to electricity-market volatility.

Why here

Why the north

The cause is congestion. Northern hydro and wind cannot move south fast enough, so the north clears cheap and the south clears expensive.

Site selection is the highest-leverage decision: power is 60–80% of the cost base of any Compute business. First plots sit in Hälsingland, on the surplus side of that line.

  1. 01

    Congestion, not a good year

    Northern surplus sits where the grid cannot move it. Kelvin sits the load at the pocket.

  2. 02

    At generation

    Generation paired with modular Compute. Industrial and municipal hosts that cannot pause on their own.

  3. 03

    Not a new feeder

    Retrofitting infrastructure at the source.

Being on the right side of that line is the siting decision.
